FRC REBUILT Drive Simulator

How the game works

  • Two alliances of 3 robots compete. You drive one; your 2 teammates and the 3 opponents drive themselves.
  • Auto (20 s): every robot, including yours, drives itself.
  • Teleop: you take control. Drive into FUEL with the front roller to collect it (hopper holds 30).
  • Shoot FUEL into your own Hub funnel. Each one is 1 point, but only while your Hub is Active.
  • Hubs take turns being inactive during the four 25 s Alliance Shifts. Both are active in Transition and End Game.
  • Cross the ramps (bumps) or drive under the trench arms to reach the Neutral Zone. Scored FUEL returns to the field.
